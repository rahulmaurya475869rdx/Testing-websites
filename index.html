🎨 About the Design

The integration of the live day/night sky, real-time sun/moon positions, and automatic eclipse detection is a deliberate design choice. It creates a calm, peaceful atmosphere — a small reminder of the natural world around us, even while browsing online.

Below this sky sits a small hand-drawn home — a quiet reminder of how sustainable power works in real life. During the day, a 5kW solar panel soaks up sunlight and stores that energy in an EXIDE tubular battery. An EXIDE inverter then converts the stored DC power into AC — the same electricity that runs fans, lights, and everyday appliances at home. So when a power cut hits, this system quietly takes over: the lights stay on, the fans keep spinning, and the home carries on without a moment of darkness.

Every detail is connected — solar to battery, battery to inverter, inverter to the home. Outside, a street light softly glows over the road, and vehicles move along with their headlights switched on at night. It's a simple picture — but it says everything about what a reliable home backup really looks like.
